Why is the public catalog smaller right now?

We are treating this release as a cleanup pass. Pages with repetitive structure or incomplete support links are being refreshed before they return to the public sitemap.

Do games require downloads?

No. The featured page uses a browser build hosted with the site. The goal is fast access, short load times, and less confusion about where the content lives.

How do privacy choices work here?

The site publishes separate privacy, cookie, and terms pages. The banner below stores a simple accept or decline preference in local storage.
