What You'll Do in the Game

When you enter the game, you'll:

  • Choose a game mode (different gameplay styles)
  • Hit the road
  • In dense traffic, you'll:
    • Change lanes left and right
    • Overtake other vehicles
    • Avoid collisions
  • Try your best to:
    • Not crash
    • Go further
    • Complete objectives

Gameplay Mechanics

1. Core Gameplay: Avoiding Cars

The road is filled with many cars (slow cars, trucks, etc.). You need to constantly:

  • Find gaps
  • Change lanes quickly

Note: Once you crash, the game ends immediately!

2. High Speed + Risky Maneuvers for Points
  • Passing other cars closely (near miss)
  • Overtaking in the opposite direction

Both will give you higher scores or coins!

3. Multiple Game Modes
  • Career (Level Mode): Complete objectives like reaching checkpoints within time limits, achieving specific scores, or driving a certain distance
  • Infinite (Endless Mode): Drive as long as you can
  • Time Trial: Race against the clock
  • Free Mode: Drive freely, relax and enjoy
4. Vehicle Progression System

Earn coins through gameplay to unlock new cars or upgrade performance, such as:

  • Faster speed
  • Better handling
5. Nitro Boost System

Press a button to activate short bursts of speed for:

  • Overtaking
  • Sprinting

But use it carefully - it can lead to crashes if not handled properly!

Controls (PC)

  • ↑: Accelerate
  • ↓: Brake
  • ← →: Change lanes left/right
  • N: Nitro boost
