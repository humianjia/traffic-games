Common Questions

How do I report a bug in a game?

Use the contact form above and select "Bug Report" as the subject. Please include the game name, what happened, and what device/browser you were using.

Can I suggest a new game?

Absolutely! We love hearing game suggestions. Use the form and select "Game Suggestion" - we review all recommendations.
